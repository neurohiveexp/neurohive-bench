Text:

The Port of Halvik will replace its ship-to-shore cranes with 14 automated units, the port authority announced on Tuesday. The €220 million project, financed partly by a loan from the regional development bank, is due to be fully operational by March 2028.

According to the authority, automation will cut the average turnaround time for a container ship from 36 hours to 22 hours and allow the port to run night shifts without additional crews. The new cranes will be operated remotely from a control building on the north pier.

The dockworkers' union, which had threatened strikes last spring, signed an agreement that guarantees no layoffs until 2031. Workers whose jobs disappear will be retrained as remote operators or maintenance technicians, with the port covering the cost of training.

Environmental groups welcomed the decision because the cranes are fully electric, but local residents have raised concerns about noise from round-the-clock operations.
